# MEMBRA Prompt To Money

MEMBRA Prompt To Money is a research scaffold for turning an AI prompt, workflow, or generated artifact into a measured, compliant, provenance-backed, productized, appraisable, and finance-ready digital asset package.

This repository section is intentionally conservative: it does not execute trades, deploy contracts, issue loans, bypass marketplace rules, or guarantee profit. It provides a structured diligence and productization pipeline for lawful research, valuation, documentation, and packaging.

## Pipeline map

The folder structure follows a sequential asset lifecycle:

1. `00_prompt_origin` captures the raw prompt, intent classification, and safety scope.
2. `01_compliance` checks platform rules, legal exposure, privacy, and allow/block status.
3. `02_provenance` records creator attestation, ownership evidence, licensing, and custody chain.
4. `03_asset_creation` stores source artifacts, transformed assets, screenshots, demos, and documentation.
5. `04_measurement` defines KPI vectors for profitability, investability, liquidity, and risk.
6. `05_benchmarking` compares the asset against similar assets and produces normalized reports.
7. `06_appraisal` produces fair value, floor, ceiling, collateral value, and price justification.
8. `07_prediction` forecasts sale probability, expected price, time to sale, expiry risk, and buyer type.
9. `08_panelos` runs investor, buyer, broker, compliance, risk, and sentinel review personas.
10. `09_productization` converts the asset into a sellable offer, listing, sales copy, and packaging plan.
11. `10_market` prepares marketplace listing data, buyer graph, outreach targets, auction settings, and RFQs.
12. `11_liquidity` maps liquidity routes, instant offer estimates, bid/ask spread, depth, and venue fit.
13. `12_financing` prepares prequalification, collateral score, advance rate, lender packet, and funding options.
14. `13_payment_settlement` tracks checkout, escrow, payment status, transfer verification, and receipt records.
15. `14_delivery` packages transfer materials, onboarding, credentials policy, and delivery confirmation.
16. `15_profitability` reconciles revenue, costs, fees, taxes, and net profit.
17. `16_audit_feedback` records realized outcome, disputes, model accuracy, and benchmark feedback.
18. `17_scale_loop` stores templates, automation scripts, next pipeline notes, and optimization findings.

## Operating principles

- No mock financial guarantees.
- No private keys or secrets in repository files.
- No unauthorized use of third-party IP.
- No platform-rule bypass.
- No simulated profitability represented as realized performance.
- Backtests and dry runs must be labeled as such.
- Any financing, collateral, appraisal, or liquidity output is preliminary research unless validated by qualified professionals and counterparties.

## Intended integrations

This scaffold can be connected to local Ollama inference, Hugging Face Spaces, GitHub Actions, vector search, document generation, spreadsheet KPI workbooks, and later oracle or settlement systems.
